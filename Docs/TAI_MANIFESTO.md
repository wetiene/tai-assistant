# Tai Manifesto

This is a founding document. It explains why Tai exists and what we believe. It is not a pitch deck, not a feature list, and not an engineering specification.

Related: `Docs/README.md`, `Docs/PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md`, `Docs/PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md`, `Docs/AI_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

---

## Why Tai exists

People already know they should eat better, train more consistently, sleep enough and pay attention to how they feel. The problem is not awareness. The problem is the moment of choice — what to eat, whether to train, how hard to push, what matters today — when life is busy and information is scattered.

Most health apps respond by asking people to become operators of their own data: log more, open more screens, interpret more charts. That creates work. It rarely creates better decisions.

Tai exists because better decisions need a partner that shows up before the choice is made, remembers what matters, and speaks clearly without demanding ceremony.

---

## People don't need another tracker

The world is full of trackers. Rings, streaks, dashboards, macros, sleep scores. Tracking can be useful infrastructure. It is not the product people actually want.

When the app’s identity is “enter data and stare at numbers,” the user becomes the analyst. That is the opposite of coaching.

Tai refuses to grow by adding another silo to monitor. Tracking exists only in service of guidance.

---

## People need better decisions

Health and performance improve through a long chain of ordinary choices. Not through perfect knowledge of yesterday’s numbers.

A better decision is timely, personal, honest about uncertainty, and small enough to act on. Tai’s job is to improve those decisions — not to maximise time spent in the app.

---

## Tai exists before decisions happen

Coaching that arrives after the meal is eaten or the session is skipped is mostly commentary.

Tai aims to be present in the window where a suggestion can still change behaviour: what to do next, what to notice, what to skip, what to adjust. Proactivity without context is noise. Context without timing is trivia. Tai needs both.

---

## The best technology disappears into the background

People should feel helped, not operated.

Interfaces, models, integrations and Memory should reduce friction until the experience feels like a calm relationship — not a dashboard to administer. When technology insists on being seen, it usually means the user is doing the system’s work.

---

## AI should remove effort

AI that creates more taps, more confirmation theatre, or more things to babysit has failed.

The point of intelligence in Tai is less effort for the same or better outcome: faster capture, fewer blank forms, less re-explaining, fewer dead ends. Effort removed must not become control removed. Recommendations can be light. Authority stays with the user.

---

## Tai remembers

A coach that forgets is a search box with a personality.

Tai remembers meals that matter, corrections the user made, goals in play, plans they own, and the shape of their life as they share it. Memory is not surveillance theatre. It is continuity — the difference between “an app” and “my coach.”

---

## Tai learns

Remembering is storage. Learning is improvement.

When someone corrects a portion, rejects a Recommendation, or prefers a way of training, Tai should get better — visibly, correctably, without silently rewriting Confirmed Artifacts. Learning earns its place only when it reduces future effort or raises the quality of the next Recommendation.

---

## Tai earns trust

Trust is not claimed in onboarding copy. It is earned in behaviour.

Tai earns trust by explaining Recommendations with Evidence, showing Confidence instead of fake precision, never silently changing programs or logs, preferring Trusted Integrations over duplicate busywork, and staying calm when engagement tactics would be easier.

Trust compounds slowly and breaks quickly. We build for the compound.

---

## Users remain in control

Tai may interpret, recommend and remind. Users confirm, correct, dismiss and delete.

Important changes to meals, goals, workouts and preferences require User Decision. The user owns their programs, their data and their boundaries. Assistance that cannot be refused is not assistance.

---

## Health is personal

Bodies, schedules, cultures, kitchens, injuries and motivations differ. Generic slogans and one-size plans pretend otherwise.

Tai treats personal context as the product substrate: what this person eats, how they train, what they have authorised, what they have corrected. Personal does not mean invasive. Privacy, proportionate permissions and clear Memory controls are part of respect.

---

## Simplicity beats feature count

Every feature asks for attention. Attention is finite.

We add capability only when it strengthens coaching — better decisions, less effort, more continuity. Navigation stays minimal. Surfaces stay few. A smaller product that is deeply useful beats a large product that is shallowly impressive.

---

## Calm beats urgency

Health is not a slot machine. Artificial urgency, streak panic and notification pressure trade long-term trust for short-term opens.

Tai should feel focused, premium and quiet enough to live with. When Tai speaks, it should be because something useful is at stake — not because a growth metric is hungry.

---

## Long-term consistency beats short-term optimisation

Dramatic hacks and aggressive targets make compelling screenshots. They make poor companions.

Tai favours guidance people can live with for years: sustainable training progression, honest nutrition estimates, recovery that respects limits, and advice that prefers safety over spectacle. Consistency is the strategy. Optimisation is a servant of that strategy, never the master.

---

## What Tai is not

- **Not a calorie counting obsession.** Numbers can inform. They must not become the identity of the product or the measure of the person.
- **Not a social network.** Health is not content for an audience. Tai is a private coaching relationship.
- **Not a notification machine.** Proactive only when contextual, permissioned and worth interrupting a life for.
- **Not a medical diagnosis tool.** Tai does not diagnose, treat or replace clinical care. Safety beats cleverness.
- **Not a generic chatbot.** Conversation is how Tai coaches in context — not an aimless chat toy disconnected from real plans and Confirmed Artifacts.
- **Not a form-based data entry app.** If the main experience is filling fields, we have rebuilt a tracker and failed our purpose.

---

## Our North Star

Tai should become the trusted AI partner that helps people make healthier decisions throughout their lives while requiring less effort every year because it continuously learns, remembers and adapts.
